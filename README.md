CONCLUSION :
The Telemedicine Management System project aims to provide a convenient and accessible platform for users to connect with doctors and manage their healthcare needs online. The project focuses on simplifying healthcare services through features such as user registration and login, doctor search, appointment booking, online consultations, medicine access, and personal health management. The Figma prototype was designed to demonstrate the application's interface, navigation, and overall user experience.
During the project, the design was evaluated through usability testing with five participants, comparing existing healthcare websites with the modified prototype. The feedback helped identify strengths and areas for improvement, including doctor information, readability, appointment-slot selection, and confirmation messages. These findings can guide further refinement of the interface and improve its usability and accessibility.
Overall, this project provided practical experience in user-centered design, prototyping, usability evaluation, and iterative improvement. The proposed system has the potential to make healthcare services easier to access and navigate. Future development could include secure user authentication, database integration, real-time appointment scheduling, video consultations, electronic prescriptions, and secure management of medical records to transform the prototype into a functional telemedicine application.
